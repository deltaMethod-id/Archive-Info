# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and considerate
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks
- Public or private intimidation
- Unwelcome sexual attention or any form of abusive behavior

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and enforcing them fairly.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement
Violations may be reported to the project maintainers and will be reviewed and addressed appropriately.
